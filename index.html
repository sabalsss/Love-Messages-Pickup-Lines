Privacy Policy

App: Flirt (package com.sabalapps.pickuplines)
Last updated: June 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Flirt mobile application (the “App”) handles your information. Your privacy matters, and the App is designed to need as little of your data as possible — in fact, none.

1. We do not collect any personal data

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data. We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the App.

2. The App works fully offline

All content is generated and stored locally on your device. The App makes no network requests and does not even request the Internet permission. There is no server or backend that receives your information.

3. Data stored on your device

Features such as Favorites and History save your selections locally on your device only. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data.

4. No analytics, no ads, no tracking

The App contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data. We do not use cookies or any tracking technologies.

5. Permissions

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive device permission.

6. Children’s privacy

The App contains light-hearted, flirtatious, and dating-themed content and is intended for a teen and adult audience. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

7. Third-party links / sharing

If you choose to share a line using your device’s share feature, the content you share is handled by the app you select (e.g., a messaging app) under that app’s own privacy policy. Flirt does not control or receive that information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
