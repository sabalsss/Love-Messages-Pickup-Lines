This Privacy Policy explains how the Flirt mobile application (the “App”), published by Sabal Chaulagain (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), accesses, collects, uses, and shares user data. Your privacy matters, and the App is designed to need as little of your data as possible — in fact, none.

1. Data we access, collect, use, and share

The App does not access, collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data, and it does not share any data with third parties. We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the App. No personal or sensitive data of any type is collected.

2. The App works fully offline

All content is generated and stored locally on your device. The App makes no network requests and does not even request the Internet permission. There is no server or backend that receives your information.

3. Data stored on your device

Features such as Favorites and History save your selections locally on your device only. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or to any third party.

4. Data retention and deletion

Because no data is collected or transmitted to us, we have no user data to retain or delete on any server. The only data the App stores is your local Favorites and History, kept on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. You can delete this data at any time by clearing History in the App, removing items from Favorites, or by uninstalling the App, which permanently deletes all App data from your device.

5. Security

Since the App does not transmit any data off your device, your information is not exposed to network interception. Data stored locally is protected by your device’s built-in security (such as your screen lock and the operating system’s app sandbox).

6. No analytics, no ads, no tracking

The App contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data. We do not use cookies or any tracking technologies.

7. Permissions

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive device permission.

8. Children’s privacy

The App contains light-hearted, flirtatious, and dating-themed content and is intended for a teen and adult audience. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

9. Third-party links / sharing

If you choose to share a line using your device’s share feature, the content you share is handled by the app you select (e.g., a messaging app) under that app’s own privacy policy. Flirt does not control or receive that information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
